## Option B″ — "Why" *(current)*

The biographical facts are deliberately **not** in the Corridor: Document Mode's hero
already introduced Rik (ADR-024), and the `RIK` sculpture (Act 3) delivers the name, so
copy that repeats either would steal its moment. The fast-learner and chaos-comfortable
traits are shown in the Market (hackathon stalls), not claimed here.

**Corridor lines — three, bare:**

1. I like problems I don't understand yet.
2. I don't stop at getting something to work. I want to know why it works.
3. Everything ahead of you started as something I didn't understand.

Line 3 does the forward-pointing structural work (the Market and Mirrors are literally
ahead) and is the "slightly uncomfortable" beat Act 2 asks for. No idiom, no aphorism.

**One-line bio** (Document Mode header)
> Rik Mukherji — computing science student at the University of Alberta, currently
> interning on AWS Outposts. I like problems I don't understand yet.

**Paragraph bio** (Document Mode intro)
> I'm a computing science student at the University of Alberta, currently interning on
> AWS Outposts. I like working on things I don't understand yet, and I don't stop once
> they work — I want to know why they work. I pick things up quickly, and I'm most
> comfortable when things are unfamiliar and moving fast; three years of 72-hour

If any line still reads machine-written, cut or simplify it — say less rather than
